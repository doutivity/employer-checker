Why check an employer?

A job offer tells you what the company wants you to know. Before you sign, it is worth looking at what others say and what public records show. Employer Checker builds one Google site: search per source, so you can review everything in a few minutes.

  • Legal & registries: Opendatabot, YouControl, Estonian e-Äriregister, UK Companies House, OpenCorporates, SEC EDGAR.
  • Employee reviews & salaries: Glassdoor, Indeed, Blind, Levels.fyi, Kununu, DOU.
  • Clients & funding: Clutch, Upwork, GoodFirms, Crunchbase, Dealroom, PitchBook, Y Combinator.
  • Engineering & social: GitHub, GitLab, Hacker News, Reddit, LinkedIn.

The tool is free and open source. Nothing you type is stored on a server, and the query lives only in the page URL so you can share it.
